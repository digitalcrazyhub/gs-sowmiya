03.

Contracts & Milestone Billing

All active construction projects are governed by a formal, stamped Construction Agreement outlining the exact built-up area, material specifications, delivery schedule, and milestone payment schedule.

Our milestone billing model ensures absolute financial peace of mind. Payments are invoiced strictly upon certified completion of defined structural milestones:

  1. Advance / Mobilization: Soil investigation, architectural approval, site leveling, and temporary power/water setup.
  2. Foundation & Substructure: Earth excavation, anti-termite treatment, PCC, RCC footing casting, and plinth beam tying.
  3. Superstructure Columns & Brickwork: Column casting up to sill and lintel levels, solid block/fly ash masonry.
  4. Roof Slab Pour: Shuttering, Fe-550 rebar binding, electrical conduit laying, and concrete pour with cube sampling.
  5. Internal Finishing & Plastering: Cement plastering, wall putty, electrical wiring, and plumbing concealed lines.
  6. Flooring & Fixtures: Premium vitrified tile installation, bathroom sanitaryware, and door/window fittings.
  7. Final Handover: Final coat painting, deep site clean, and ceremonial key handover.
Timely Milestone Settlement

Construction milestones require prompt payment within seven (7) banking days of invoice delivery to prevent disruption to labor scheduling, raw material deliveries, and continuous concrete pours.

04.

Materials & Quality Standards

With 15+ years of family construction heritage guided by Er. Suresh Kumar and our founder mentor, GS Sowmiya Builders maintains uncompromising material integrity:

  • Structural Steel: Certified primary Fe-500D or Fe-550 grade TMT rebars (Tata Tiscon, JSW Steel, or equivalent ISI-marked brands) free from atmospheric corrosion.
  • Cement & Ready-Mix: 53-grade and 43-grade OPC / PPC cement (UltraTech, Ramco, Coromandel) tested for initial setting time and fineness.
  • Concrete Batching: Standard M20 and M25 controlled concrete mixes. Compressive cube tests are conducted at 7-day and 28-day curing intervals, and results are shared openly with the client.
  • Plumbing & Electrical: CPVC/PVC concealed conduits (Ashirvad, Astral) and fire-retardant copper wiring (Finolex, Polycab) with modular switches.

Any client-requested material substitution or specification upgrade must be recorded in writing through a formal Change Order detailing cost and timeline implications.

08.

Warranty & Defect Liability Period

We stand by the endurance and craftsmanship of our built spaces:

  • Structural Warranty: We provide a comprehensive structural warranty covering the RCC frame (footings, columns, plinth beams, and roof slabs) against structural failure arising from construction defects.
  • Defect Liability Period (DLP): For twelve (12) months following ceremonial handover, our maintenance team rectifies any non-wear-and-tear defects involving plumbing leaks, electrical connections, or plaster settlement cracks free of labor charges.

Exclusions: The warranty does not cover damages caused by unauthorized structural alterations executed by third-party workers, intentional vandalism, inadequate client maintenance, or severe force majeure events.

09.

Limitation of Liability & Force Majeure

Neither party shall be held liable for construction delays or temporary work stoppages caused by events beyond reasonable human control ("Force Majeure"), including:

  • Severe monsoon inundation, tropical cyclones, or earthquakes officially declared natural calamities.
  • Unforeseen regional disruptions in sand, river aggregate, or cement quarrying mandated by judicial orders or governmental moratoria.
  • Statutory planning or municipal sanction delays arising from changes in municipal master plans or governmental administrative shutdowns.

In such events, the project completion timeline shall be automatically extended by a period proportional to the duration of the Force Majeure occurrence.

Dispute Resolution & Jurisdiction

  1. Amicable Negotiation: The parties commit to resolving the matter amicably through direct, good-faith dialogue between the client and the Managing Director of GS Sowmiya Builders Private Limited.
  2. Arbitration: If unresolved within thirty (30) days, the dispute shall be referred to sole arbitration conducted in Chennai under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The proceedings shall be conducted in English or Tamil.
  3. Governing Jurisdiction: The courts situated in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India shall have sole and exclusive jurisdiction over any legal matters arising under these Terms.
