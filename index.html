About GS Sowmiya Builders

About Us

GS Sowmiya Builders Private Limited is a second-generation construction firm in Chennai. Founded by civil engineer Er. Suresh Kumar and guided by senior construction mentor Mr. Gurusamy—who brings over 15+ years of frontline building contracting experience across Tamil Nadu.

We believe that owning a structurally sound, beautifully finished home shouldn't be a privilege reserved for the few. From our premier 2 BHK residences at GS Sowmiya Elite Enclave in Medavakkam to bespoke custom villas, joint ventures, and turnkey contracts, we execute every project with earthquake-resistant RCC engineering, grade-tested materials, and zero hidden costs.
