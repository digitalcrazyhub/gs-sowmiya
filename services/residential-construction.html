  • Client brief & lifestyle discovery

  • Budget & spatial feasibility check

PLAN

  • Brick masonry & weather-resistant plaster

MONITOR

  • Cube compression & slump testing
  • MEP pressure testing for leak prevention
  • Weekly photo & video progress reports
06 HANDOVER

COMPLETE

The completed work is reviewed, finishing requirements are addressed and the project is prepared for handover.

  • Snag-list verification audit
  • Deep site cleaning & key ceremonial delivery
  • Comprehensive as-built drawings & warranties

Civil & Structural Works

Engineered foundations, reinforced concrete frames, lintels, roof slabs, and load-tested structural columns conforming strictly to Indian Standards.

  • Computerized ready-mix or on-site batching

Electrical Works

Concealed fire-retardant electrical conduit routing, distribution boards, earthing systems, EV charging provisions, and smart lighting infrastructure.

Plumbing Works

Multi-line water distribution, CPVC/UPVC piping, acoustic wastewater lines, rainwater harvesting filtration, and zero-leakage sump connections.
