Interior & Woodwork FAQs

Clear answers regarding material durability, execution timelines, warranties, and construction coordination.

The optimal time is during the brick masonry and electrical conduit grooving stage. Finalizing wardrobe lengths, kitchen counter positions, and TV consoles at this point allows us to place electrical switch sockets, plumbing inlets, and chimney core cuts exactly where needed without damaging finished plaster later.

Chennai's coastal climate and humid conditions cause particle board and standard MDF to swell and degrade quickly when exposed to moisture and daily Indian cooking. Boiling Water Proof (BWP 710) marine plywood is chemically treated, termite-proof, and can withstand prolonged boiling water exposure without delamination.

For a typical 3 BHK home (modular kitchen, 3 wardrobes with lofts, TV unit, foyer partition, false ceiling, and lighting), complete execution takes approximately 35 to 50 working days once floor tiling and wall plastering are cured and ready.
