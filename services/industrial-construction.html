Industrial Construction FAQ

Clear answers regarding pre-engineered buildings, heavy floor load tolerances, and industrial safety certifications.

Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEBs) provide 30-50% faster construction speeds, wide column-free clear spans of up to 60 meters, superior seismic resistance, and lower overall foundation load requirements compared to conventional RCC.

We construct floors engineered for uniformly distributed loads (UDL) from 5 tons/m² to over 15 tons/m², along with high point-load capacities for heavy pallet racking uprights and heavy counterbalanced forklifts.

Yes, we engineer structural portal frames with stepped columns and heavy crane runway beams capable of supporting single and double-girder EOT cranes from 5 tons up to 50 tons capacity.

Yes, our engineering team manages complete statutory liaison for Directorate of Industrial Safety & Health (DISH) factory plan approvals, Fire NOCs, TNPCB consent to establish (CTE), and TANGEDCO industrial power sanctions.

We construct separate mass-concrete foundation blocks resting on vibration-isolation pads (nitrile or cork elastomers) surrounded by perimeter expansion joints to completely decouple machine vibrations from the main building frame.
