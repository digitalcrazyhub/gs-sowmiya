Our Architectural Design Process

A structured five-stage workflow ensuring every design decision is collaborative, practical, and fully constructible.

01

Site Analysis & Client Brief

We study plot orientation, road width, prevailing breezes, solar path, and discuss your family's space requirements and budget.

02

Concept & 2D Space Layouts

We draft multiple layout options balancing room proportions, Vastu alignments, natural ventilation, and circulation flow.

03

3D Elevation & Material Selection

We model high-resolution 3D facades presenting exterior textures, colors, glass elements, lighting, and facade treatments.

04

Structural & Services Detailing

Structural engineers prepare RCC beam-column drawings alongside electrical conduit and plumbing coordinate diagrams.

05

Build-Ready Dossier & Handover

We compile a comprehensive working drawing dossier and BOQ estimation ready for municipal approvals and seamless site construction.
