Residential Construction FAQs

Clear answers to common questions about house construction timelines, approvals, payments, and site management in Chennai.

Before breaking ground in Chennai, you need building plan approval from the Greater Chennai Corporation (GCC) or CMDA/DTCP depending on the jurisdiction. The submission requires clear title deeds, Patta, FMB sketch, structural engineering drawings, and architectural plan layouts. Our team coordinates the documentation and submission process on your behalf.

For a typical 2,500 to 4,000 sq.ft. independent house or villa (G+1 or G+2), construction generally takes between 8 and 12 months. This includes foundation excavation, curing periods for each concrete slab (minimum 21 days), brickwork, plastering, interior tiling, electrical/plumbing works, and exterior weather coating.

Yes, absolutely. If you have already engaged an architect who has prepared architectural drawings and structural details, we can act purely as your civil contractor. Our site engineers review the drawings for buildability, prepare a detailed Bill of Quantities (BOQ), and execute the project strictly according to your architect's specifications.

We use a strictly milestone-based payment structure divided into 7 to 9 transparent stages (e.g., booking advance, plinth level completion, ground floor roof slab, first floor roof slab, brickwork, plastering, flooring, and final handover). You never pay for uncompleted work—each installment is released only after site verification.

Our qualified site engineers perform on-site slump cone tests during casting and cast concrete test cubes that are tested at 7 and 28 days for compressive strength in certified laboratories. We also enforce continuous pond curing on roof slabs for 21 days and vertical column burlap curing to prevent thermal shrinkage cracks.
