A civil contractor executes the manual building work. A project management consultant works solely for the property owner as an independent technical auditor—checking that the contractor uses specified rebar grades, maintains proper concrete curing, sticks to timelines, and only bills for verified physical quantities.

We check mill test certificates (MTC) for steel shipments, verify manufacturer embossing on TMT bars, test sand for silt content, check cement bag packaging dates (ensuring cement is fresh within 90 days of manufacture), and cast concrete cubes for certified 7-day and 28-day compression tests.

You receive weekly consolidated progress reports with high-resolution site photographs, stage completion percentages, upcoming milestone forecasts, and a log of any resolved snags. We also provide instant photo updates before and during critical events like foundation and roof slab concrete pours.

If our engineers identify deviations (such as improper column verticality, honeycombing in concrete, or delayed brickwork), we issue formal Non-Conformance Reports (NCR) requiring the contractor to rectify the issue before proceeding. Payment certificates are withheld until defects are completely corrected.
