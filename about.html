The Founding Legacy

A HERITAGE OF TRUST, ROOTED IN CHENNAI

GS Sowmiya Builders Private Limited was incorporated in 2023 with a heartfelt purpose: to make quality dream homes accessible to all classes of people without compromising on structural safety or finish quality.

As second-generation builders, our company is built on over 30 years of hands-on civil contracting experience established by Mr. Gurusamy, a well-regarded building contractor who helped shape more than 200 home construction projects across Chennai through earlier contracting and real estate works.

Under the leadership of engineering graduate Er. Suresh Kumar, GS Sowmiya Builders infuses that field-tested wisdom with modern structural engineering standards, transparent itemized BOQs, RERA compliance, and direct principal supervision on every site.

“Our commitment is to construct each home as if our own family were to live there — with honest pricing, uncompromising structural strength, and lasting peace of mind.”

— Er. Suresh Kumar, Founder & Managing Director

Our Principles

FOUR PILLARS OF OUR DISCIPLINE

The core structural and ethical principles that guide every foundation we excavate and every key we hand over.

01

Structural Rigor

We strictly specify primary producer Fe-550D TMT steel and Grade-53 cement. Every column and slab is verified for proper cover, tie spacing, and slump.

02

Vaastu & Natural Light

Every home plan balances authentic Vaastu Shastra compliance with generous cross-ventilation, abundant sunlight, and functional modern room flow.

03

Itemized BOQ Transparency

Clear per-square-foot cost breakdowns, material brand specifications, stage milestone billing, and absolute zero hidden escalation clauses.

04

Hands-On Supervision

Direct on-site presence by qualified civil engineers and founder Er. Suresh Kumar. No unaccountable subcontracting layers between you and the builder.
