Official Corporate Disclosure

GS Sowmiya Builders Private Limited (CIN: U43299TN2023PTC161774), registered under the Companies Act, 2013, operates as a premier building construction and property development entity based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, storage, and safeguarding of information obtained through our official website (https://gssowmiyabuilders.com), direct consultations, site visits, property joint venture proposals, and contractual construction engagements across Chennai and the wider Tamil Nadu region.

01.

Introduction & Corporate Scope

We recognize that embarking on a home construction, joint venture, or renovation project is one of the most significant emotional and financial investments a family or landowner makes. Respecting your personal privacy, confidentiality of title records, and financial details is fundamental to our operational ethos.

This policy applies to all prospective homebuyers, existing clients, plot owners in joint venture negotiations, subcontractors, and visitors interacting with our digital touchpoints or visiting our registered office at No 106, Nallasamy Tower, Velachery Main Road, Pallikaranai, Chennai - 600 100.

02.

Information We Collect

To provide accurate structural feasibility assessments, transparent Bill of Quantities (BOQ) estimates, and milestone construction tracking, we collect information in the following categories:

  • Personal Contact Details: Full name, telephone number, WhatsApp contact number, email address, and residential postal address provided during project enquiries or booking reservations.
  • Plot & Property Specifications: Survey numbers, patta / chitta details, land dimensions, geographical location (e.g., Medavakkam, Vengaivasal, Pallikaranai, Tambaram, Vignarajapuram), soil inspection records, and existing building parameters required for plan sanctions and structural calculations.
  • Architectural & Engineering Requirements: Custom floor plan preferences, vastu specifications, room requirements, structural preferences (e.g., Fe-550 TMT rebars, M20/M25 concrete mix grades), and interior finishing choices.
  • Financial & Contractual Records: Milestone payment receipts, banking transaction references for stage-wise construction clearances, PAN / Aadhaar copies strictly as mandated by statutory compliance (e.g., CMDA / DTCP plan approvals, stamp duty registration, and RERA disclosures).
  • Technical Usage Logs: IP address, device type, browser characteristics, and page engagement data gathered automatically to ensure website performance and security.
No Unsolicited Data Harvesting

We do not purchase marketing databases, run cold algorithmic scraping, or collect personal credentials without your explicit, voluntary submission.

03.

How We Utilize Your Data

Information collected is utilized exclusively for legitimate construction, architectural, and business operational purposes, including:

  1. Project Feasibility & BOQ Formulation: Evaluating site dimensions, calculating steel and concrete requirements, preparing transparent cost breakdowns, and customizing architectural elevations.
  2. Statutory Planning Sanctions: Submitting building plan applications, structural drawings, and ownership proofs to municipal bodies including CMDA, DTCP, and Greater Chennai Corporation under client power of attorney or joint venture authorization.
  3. Construction Milestone Communication: Transmitting site progress photographs, slump test certificates, cube strength testing reports, and stage-wise billing notifications.
  4. Customer Care & Warranty Administration: Managing post-handover inspections during the defect liability period and addressing ongoing maintenance queries.
  5. Legal & Tax Compliance: Maintaining audit-ready records in accordance with Indian taxation laws (GST, TDS) and company regulatory frameworks.
04.

Non-Disclosure & Third-Party Sharing Policy

GS Sowmiya Builders Private Limited enforces an absolute zero-commercialization rule: We never sell, rent, monetize, or barter your personal records, telephone numbers, or property details to third-party telemarketers, real estate aggregators, or advertising brokers.

Information is disclosed strictly under the following controlled circumstances:

  • Licensed Technical Partners: Empaneled structural engineers, chartered architects, geotech soil testing laboratories, and MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing) consultants bound by confidentiality agreements for the execution of your specific project.
  • Statutory Regulatory Authorities: Government planning departments, municipal commissioners, registrars, and revenue authorities when legally mandated for plan sanctions, land approvals, or tax compliance.
  • Financial Institutions (Upon Request): Housing finance corporations (e.g., SBI, HDFC, LIC Housing Finance) solely when authorized by the homebuyer to facilitate home loan disbursements and stage inspection approvals.
Strict Landowner Confidentiality in Joint Ventures

Landowner negotiations, ratio share agreements, and preliminary site sketches remain private between the landowner and our leadership team led by Er. Suresh Kumar.

05.

Data Protection & Technical Safety

We deploy multi-layered physical, operational, and digital safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, data alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS): All digital transmissions between your web browser and our website are encrypted using industry-standard 256-bit SSL/TLS protocols.
  • Restricted Internal Access: Physical files, structural calculations, and client agreements are restricted to authorized engineering and accounting personnel on a strict need-to-know basis.
  • Secure Electronic Storage: Digital records and communication backups are hosted on enterprise cloud servers featuring automated firewalls, intrusion detection, and regular security patching.
06.

Land Titles & Blueprints Handling

We understand the sensitive nature of original and certified copies of land titles, encumbrance certificates (EC), parent documents, and architectural blueprints submitted for joint venture evaluation or turnkey civil work.

All physical documentation is cataloged in fire-resistant, secure custody at our corporate office. Documents submitted strictly for preliminary legal vetting or planning feasibility that do not culminate in a contractual engagement will be returned or securely purged upon written request.

07.

Cookies & Digital Analytics

Our website uses standard essential and analytical cookies to maintain session states, remember language preferences, and analyze aggregated traffic patterns (such as page load speeds and visitor navigation flows).

These cookies do not track personally identifiable credentials or monitor your activity across external non-affiliated websites. You may modify your browser settings at any time to reject non-essential cookies without affecting the core readability of our website.

08.

Your Rights & Access Controls

As a valued client or website user, you retain the following rights regarding your personal records under applicable Indian privacy and consumer protection laws:

  • Right to Review: Request a summary of your personal and project records stored within our active customer records.
  • Right to Rectification: Request prompt correction of outdated or inaccurate contact details, spelling variations, or billing information.
  • Right to Revoke Consent: Unsubscribe from non-essential promotional SMS, WhatsApp updates, or email circulars at any time by contacting our helpline.
  • Right to Erasure: Request the deletion of marketing records upon project completion, subject to mandatory statutory tax and legal retention requirements.
