PRIVACY POLICY

Information about how this website handles details shared with GS Sowmiya Builders Private Limited.

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Introduction

This policy explains how information is handled when you visit this website or contact GS Sowmiya Builders Private Limited through it.

Information We Collect

We may receive information you choose to provide through an enquiry, including your name, phone number, email address, project location, service interest, and message.

Website Usage Information

The website may receive ordinary technical information such as browser, device, approximate usage, and pages visited when optional analytics is enabled with consent.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to review project enquiries, respond to requests, communicate about relevant services, maintain the website, and meet applicable obligations.

Contact and Enquiry Information

Enquiry details are intended for communication about the request submitted. Do not submit information that is not needed to discuss your project.

Cookies and Analytics

This site does not load optional analytics unless a measurement ID is configured and you give consent. Necessary browser storage may be used to remember that choice.

Third-Party Services

Some pages link to or embed services such as WhatsApp, Google Maps, Google Fonts, and image hosting. Those services have their own policies and may process requests when you use them.

Data Security and Retention

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled through the website. Enquiry information is retained only as long as needed for the enquiry, business records, or legal obligations.

User Rights

You may contact us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of personal information shared through this website, subject to applicable legal requirements.

External Links

Links to external websites are provided for convenience. Their content and privacy practices are controlled by those providers.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when the website, services, or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the latest version.
